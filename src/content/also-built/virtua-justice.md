An on-rails light gun shooter, written as an original tribute to the arcade grammar of Virtua Cop 2. Threat reticles that shrink as an enemy's timer runs down, hit zones, weapon disarms that pay a bonus for not killing anyone, civilians you have to not shoot, and a three-phase boss. 2,325 lines in one HTML file. Every piece of geometry is built from Three.js primitives at runtime and all the audio is synthesised, so there isn't a single model, texture or sound file in the repository.
